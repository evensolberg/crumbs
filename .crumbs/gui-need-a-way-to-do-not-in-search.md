# GUI: Need a way to do NOT in search

In most search engine "-term" means "not term". Can we do something similar? I may want to see all crumbs NOT relating to a certain subject.

We could use - or ! perhaps.

Further down the road, we should enable boolean search, e.g., "term1 AND (term2 OR term3) NOT term4"
